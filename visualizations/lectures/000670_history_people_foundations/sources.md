## Image attribution

[The committed manifest](../../dkvis/assets/history-images.yaml) records exact
Commons file pages, creators or credited collections, chosen license and URL,
upstream image revision, and changes. Every displayed portrait has an on-slide
credit; the final credit cards retain source and license URLs. Selected original or standard Wikimedia thumbnail renditions
are kept without a new crop. Already-cropped source images are identified in
the manifest. The ignored store retains the selected image bytes and a JSON source
snapshot together, including download time and duration.

Public-domain entries are labelled public domain, not mislabelled as CC grants.
CC BY-SA photographs retain their own licenses. The manifest and accompanying
records preserve those terms when the image files are shared independently.
This lecture does not claim ownership of the original photographs.

## Scope

Publication dates describe publications, not an unverified first-discovery date.
Career places are not claimed as the locations where a particular paper was
written. No quotation or personal conversation has been invented. We do not
infer a researcher's motivation from a portrait. Other contributors and
uncleared portrait leads remain in [the cast and image notes](../../docs/planning/history-cast-and-images.md).
