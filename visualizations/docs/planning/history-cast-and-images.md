# Historical cast and image coverage

5 November 2026. This is an editorial coverage note, not a theorem census or a
claim to have enumerated every contributor to spectral theory. It compares the
current lecture cast with the manuscript's historical exposition and the
publication-direction references. A deferred name is not a missing Lean proof.

## Built now

- `history-overview` (000505): the mathematical overview, now with an explicit
  James Joseph Sylvester scene and portrait before the operator-equation story.
- `history-people-foundations` (000670): Sylvester, Jordan, Rayleigh, Ritz, Weyl.
- `history-people-operators` (000835): Sz.-Nagy, Halmos, Kato, Davis, Kahan.

The people episodes introduce careers and institutions, one relevant
publication, and one mathematical connection per person. They do not reteach
proofs. They distinguish portrait dates, publication dates, and documented
collaborations. A modern compound name does not imply a joint publication.

## Image decisions and concise triage

The committed [manifest](../../dkvis/assets/history-images.yaml) selects ten
images from Wikimedia Commons. Each file's own license was inspected; the
Commons site's general text license was not used as a substitute for the
image license. Public-domain portraits are labelled public domain. Modern
photographs were selected under CC BY or CC BY-SA, including the explicitly
CC BY-SA re-scans/crops of Kato, Halmos, and Kahan, rather than older GFDL-only
versions of similar photographs.

The asset store keeps the selected original or standard Commons thumbnail,
its exact source record, and a checksum. A thumbnail has its own downloaded
checksum as well as the original image revision reference. That is a small
integrity record, not a content-addressed storage framework. No image bytes
are checked into Git.

Remaining review is narrow:

- Listen for TTS pronunciation of Sylvester, Sz.-Nagy, Ritz, Weyl, and Kahan.
- Later cast members still need selected portraits or a suitable licensed
  supporting image. Absence here means no cleared selection, not a claim that
  no image exists anywhere.
- Original Courant–Fischer publication dating and exact transport priority
  remain separate historical questions from the existing source references.

restoration. See each people lecture's `sources.md` for biographical links.
