We also formalize the principal Yu--Wang--Samworth (YWS)
population-gap inequalities and their singular-subspace extension. This
specialization tests the general theory in a setting with discrete spectra
and statistical eigenvalue-gap hypotheses; its source-version and boundary
conventions are treated in Appendix~\ref{app:yws-coverage}. The complete
Davis--Kahan result correspondence appears in
Appendix~\ref{app:dk-inventory}, with the review method in
Appendix~\ref{app:source-review}.
