## YWS terminology

The 2015 versions we inspected contain two previously highlighted printed
issues, recorded in `YWS_VERSION_RECORD.md`. These are not an exhaustive
list of the source-audit concerns; the census also records Theorem 1 endpoint
conventions and the standalone Corollary 1 normalization omission.
Do not refer to these as “two false YWS theorems.” The two highlighted
issues are:

1. equation (4) is missing a square; and
2. Theorem 3's printed rank-boundary convention uses `rank(A)+1` where the
   argument requires the ambient singular/eigenvalue index together with the
   ordinary zero continuation of singular values.

Several theorem-facing census rows depend on corrections or strengthened
hypotheses. The number of rows marked corrected is not the count of
independent defects. Preprint-to-publication corrections must be recorded by
edition; whether a later-than-2015 publisher PDF revised the two formulas
has not been established.

Reserve “false proposition” for Davis--Kahan Proposition 4.4, where the
repository contains an explicit formal counterexample to the printed claim.
