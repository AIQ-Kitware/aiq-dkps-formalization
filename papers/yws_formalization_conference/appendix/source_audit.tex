\section{YWS source-audit scope}
\label{app:yws-coverage}

The YWS audit uses a separate denominator, because this article does not
claim a line-by-line formalization of every statement in
\citet{yu2015davis}. The maintained census has \YWSCensusEntryCount{} entries:
\YWSCensusPaperSourceCount{} associated with the printed article and
\YWSCensusExplicitAdditionCount{} explicit additions. All
\YWSCensusVerifiedCount{} entries have verified Lean evidence. The
source entries include Theorems~1--3, their main consequences, and
supporting identities and examples. The
\YWSCensusCorrectedCount{} entries classified as corrected include multiple
formal results affected by the same source convention; they do not count
independent defects.

The archive of the 2015 \emph{Biometrika} advance-access article (printed
p.~4, equation~(4)) gives a double-angle identity without one required
square. For real unit vectors $u,v$, the identity proved in Lean is
\begin{equation}
  (2\langle v,u\rangle)^2(1-\langle v,u\rangle^2)
  =\frac14\|v-u\|^2(2-\|v-u\|^2)^2(4-\|v-u\|^2).
  \label{eq:yws-equation4-corrected}
\end{equation}
A checked counterexample refutes the displayed 2015 version, and a
separate theorem proves the corrected identity for unit vectors.

The same article (printed p.~6, Theorem~3) places the terminal convention
$\sigma^2_{\operatorname{rank}(A)+1}:=-\infty$ immediately after the rank.
For a rectangular or rank-deficient matrix, the Gram spectrum has zeros
between the rank and its ambient dimension. At the rank boundary the
printed convention can produce an infinite denominator and a false zero
bound on the distance between singular subspaces. The formalization
includes a counterexample in dimension two and proves the bound with the
terminal convention at the ambient spectral index.

The source review also records other edition and hypothesis differences,
including corrected indices between the 2014 preprint and the 2015 article,
endpoint conventions in the latter's Theorem~1, and omitted unit-vector
normalization in the standalone Corollary~1. The corresponding Lean
statements make the intended mathematical conditions explicit. The claims
above concern the inspected 2015 advance-access and author-accepted texts;
they do not assert that every subsequent printing contains the same
formulas.
