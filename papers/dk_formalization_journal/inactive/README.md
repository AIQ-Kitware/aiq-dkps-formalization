# Inactive TeX sources

These are preserved research or manuscript drafts, **not** inputs to the
current journal article (`../paper.tex`) and **not** part of its Overleaf or
publisher exports. Files were moved here without changing their contents.

- `paper_working.tex`: retired wrapper around the main paper.
- `appendix_transport_provenance.tex`: former supplementary provenance section.
- `real_four_dimensional_transport.tex`, `real_matching_transport.tex`: older real transport drafts.
- `sharp_displacement.tex`, `sharp_displacement_verified.tex`, `sharp_displacement_unformalized.tex`: earlier displacement treatments.
- `schatten_optimization.tex`, `schatten_transport_verified.tex`: earlier Schatten optimization treatments.
- `smoothed_transport.tex`, `smoothed_transport_verified.tex`: earlier smoothed transport treatments.
- `nuclear_endpoint_verified.tex`: earlier nuclear endpoint treatment.
- `spectral_penalty_transport.tex`, `unified_spectral_transport.tex`: earlier generalized spectral transport drafts.

Some dated research notes in the parent directory refer to these files by
their original names or describe earlier inclusion graphs. Those notes are
historical, not the current build definition. For maintained transport research,
see `../../optimal_spectral_transport/`.
