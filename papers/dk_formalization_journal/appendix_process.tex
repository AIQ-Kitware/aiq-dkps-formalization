\section{Source-correspondence review}
\label{app:source-review}

The review compares each selected source statement with its Lean counterpart,
including hypotheses, scalar field, operator domains, spectral-gap cases, and
norm conventions.  The public sine-theta theorem uses the positive sine
operator; \Cref{eq:modulus-sine} identifies it with the modulus of the
rectangular off-diagonal block used in the analytic proof.  The inventory
records Proposition~4.4 through its four-dimensional counterexample and
corrected Q-norm conclusion.
