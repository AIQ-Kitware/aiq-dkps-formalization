# Supporting research and review records

These are research notes, audits, literature maps, and historical working records
for the journal manuscript. They are **not** `\input` files and are not
included in the journal or Overleaf submission. The active authoring guidance
is in [`../docs/`](../docs/); the paper is [`../paper.tex`](../paper.tex).

Older mathematical manuscript fragments are preserved separately under
[`../inactive/`](../inactive/README.md). Supporting records here remain
available for citations, claim audits, and future editorial work.
