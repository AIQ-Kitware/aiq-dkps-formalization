\begin{proposition}[Exact trace-norm optimum over complex spaces]
\label{prop:draft-complex-trace-optimum}
If $U,V$ are equal-dimensional subspaces of a finite-dimensional complex
Hilbert space, then
\begin{equation}
  \min_{W:\,WU=V}\|I-W\|_1
  =\|P-Q\|_1
  =2\sum_{j=1}^k\sin\theta_j.
  \label{eq:draft-complex-trace-optimum}
\end{equation}
No acuteness hypothesis is needed.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The lower bound is \cref{prop:draft-projection-lower}. We construct a unitary
attaining it, including zero and right angles, without using acuteness.

Let $T=P_V|_U:U\to V$. Its finite-dimensional singular-value
decomposition supplies orthonormal bases $(u_j)_{j=1}^k$ of $U$ and
$(v_j)_{j=1}^k$ of $V$ such that
\[
 \langle u_j,v_\ell\rangle=\delta_{j\ell}c_j,
 \qquad c_j=\cos\theta_j\in[0,1].
\]
The zero singular values cause no problem: complete the nonzero left
singular vectors to an orthonormal basis of $V$, matching the remaining
right singular vectors arbitrarily. Equal dimensions make the index sets
match. When $c_j=1$, the equality case of Cauchy--Schwarz gives
$v_j=u_j$, so this index contributes a common fixed line.

For every $j$ with $s_j=\sin\theta_j>0$ put
\[
 e_j=\frac{v_j-c_j u_j}{s_j}.
\]
The overlap identities give
$\langle u_\ell,e_j\rangle=0$ for every $\ell$,
and $\langle e_j,e_\ell\rangle=\delta_{j\ell}$; in particular all
planes $K_j=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{u_j,e_j\}$ are mutually
orthogonal. Moreover $v_j=c_j u_j+s_j e_j$. This is valid at a
right angle ($c_j=0$, $e_j=v_j$) as well as at a generic angle.
The orthogonal complement of the sum of the $K_j$, together with the
zero-angle lines, is an unchanged summand on which both projections
vanish or coincide. This establishes the required \emph{finite} CS
principal-plane model explicitly.

In the ordered basis $(u_j,e_j)$ of each $K_j$, let
\[
 R_j=\begin{pmatrix}c_j&-s_j\\s_j&c_j\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad W_j=e^{i\theta_j}R_j.
\]
Here $R_ju_j=v_j$, and the complex scalar $e^{i\theta_j}$ does not
change the target line; hence $W_j(\mathbb C u_j)=\mathbb C v_j$.
The eigenvalues of $R_j$ are $e^{\pm i\theta_j}$, so those of $W_j$
are $1$ and $e^{2i\theta_j}$. Consequently
\[
 \|I-W_j\|_1=|1-e^{2i\theta_j}|=2s_j.
\]
Equivalently, after replacing $e_j$ by $-ie_j$, the same standard rotation
has matrix
\begin{equation}
 R_\theta=\begin{pmatrix}
   \cos\theta&i\sin\theta\\ i\sin\theta&\cos\theta
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad W_\theta=e^{i\theta}R_\theta.
 \label{eq:draft-complex-phase}
\end{equation}
This is the phase-modified matrix used in the formalized complex planar
construction. In the original basis it maps the source line to the target
line exactly up to a complex phase.

Take the orthogonal sum of $W_j$ for the indices with $s_j>0$ and the
identity on the unchanged summand. The resulting ambient unitary $W$
satisfies $WU=V$. Trace norm is additive over orthogonal blocks, so
\[
 \|I-W\|_1=\sum_{j=1}^k2s_j.
\]
On $K_j$ the two rank-one orthogonal projections have difference of
square $s_j^2I_{K_j}$, with two singular values $s_j$. Their difference
vanishes on the unchanged summand. It follows that
$\|P-Q\|_1=2\sum_js_j=\|I-W\|_1$. The universal projection lower bound
then proves that this $W$ is a global minimizer.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The real four-dimensional realization.}
Identifying $\mathbb C^2$ with $\mathbb R^4$ and complex multiplication by
$i$ with $J=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$
turns \cref{eq:draft-complex-phase} into the real block matrices in
\cref{eq:wtheta}, including the canonical rotation $R_\theta$. Complex
singular values are duplicated under realification, producing the trace
norms in \cref{eq:competitor-trace,eq:direct-trace}. Davis and Kahan already
used a complex scalar phase to exhibit trace-norm nonminimality
\citep[Example~4.2]{davis1970rotation}; the realification explains why
their subsequent restriction to real spaces does not restore the asserted
minimality. Bolotnikov, Li, and Rodman later studied this norm-minimization
problem and the role of $Q$-norms in detail
\citep{bolotnikov2001minimal}.
