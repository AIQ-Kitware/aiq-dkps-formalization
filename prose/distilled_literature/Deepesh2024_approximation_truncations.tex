\section{Deepesh 2024: approximation numbers under operator truncation}
\label{sec:deepesh-2024-approximation-truncations}

\paragraph{Bibliographic record.}
Deepesh K. P., ``Approximation results on s-numbers of operators,''
\emph{arXiv:2401.08483}, 2024.

\paragraph{Role in this repository.}
This paper is the closest modern source found for the analytic limit step in
Davis--Kahan's unbounded cutoff argument.  It studies convergence of
approximation numbers for truncated operators of the form $Q_\alpha T P_\alpha$
under weak or weak-star operator convergence of contractive truncations.  The
spectral cutoffs in the formalization are orthogonal projections and therefore
fit a stronger, more structured special case.

\paragraph{Why strong convergence is insufficient by a one-line estimate.}
For a strongly convergent family of projections $P_\alpha\to I$, contractivity
immediately gives
\[
  a_n(TP_\alpha)\le a_n(T).
\]
The reverse limiting inequality cannot be obtained from
$\lVert T-TP_\alpha\rVert\to0$, because strong convergence need not be
operator-norm convergence.  One must control near-optimal finite-rank
competitors or use an equivalent min--max/compactness characterization.  This
is the substantive analytic content of the cutoff theorem.

\paragraph{Specialization needed here.}
The repository only needs Hilbert spaces, right and left orthogonal spectral
cutoffs, and each fixed finite index.  A suitable proof may therefore use:
\begin{enumerate}
\item contraction of the cutoff projections;
\item uniform convergence on each fixed finite-dimensional subspace;
\item a Hilbert-space min--max characterization of approximation numbers; and
\item passage from individual approximation numbers to a finite Ky Fan sum.
\end{enumerate}
No separability assumption should be introduced, because the Davis--Kahan
source theorem is not restricted to separable Hilbert spaces.

\paragraph{Formalization map.}
The target declaration is
\texttt{approximationSingularValue\_comp\_strongProjection\_tendsto}.
Its finite-sum corollary is
\texttt{kyFanApproximationGauge\_comp\_strongProjection\_tendsto}.
The latter should remain a short finite-sum proof once the individual-index
