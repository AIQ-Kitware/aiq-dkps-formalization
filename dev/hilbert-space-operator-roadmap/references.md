# References for the Hilbert-space operator theory family

This is a **learning companion**, not part of the normative roadmap specification. It
consolidates the references cited across the six child roadmaps, removes duplicates, and
adds stable public links where one is known. The links are intended to help a reader find
the source; access to the full text may still depend on the publisher or an institution.

The **Used in** lines name the child roadmaps that currently cite or rely on the source:

- **Foundations** — `HilbertSpaceOperatorFoundations`
- **Norms and angles** — `MajorizationAndAngles`
- **Ideals** — `OperatorIdeals`
- **Spectral theory** — `SelfAdjointSpectralTheory`
- **Perturbation** — `SpectralSubspacePerturbation`
- **Statistics** — `MatrixSpectralStatistics`

## Citation needing a more precise roadmap reference

The perturbation roadmap currently says that the Haagerup–Zsidó provenance is
“followed through the Albeverio–Makarov–Motovilov reconstruction.” That phrase does
not identify a unique work. A closely related publicly available paper is:

S. Albeverio, K. A. Makarov, and A. K. Motovilov, “Graph Subspaces and the Spectral
Shift Function,” *Canadian Journal of Mathematics* **55** (2003), 449–503.

- arXiv: <https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0105142>
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-2003-020-7>

This entry should not be treated as confirmation that it is the exact intended source
for the `π / 2` provenance. The perturbation roadmap should eventually name the exact
Albeverio–Makarov–Motovilov source it means.

## How to use this list

For an initial pass through the family, a practical reading order is:

1. Horn–Johnson for finite-dimensional self-adjoint operators, square roots,
   Courant–Fischer, and polar decomposition.
2. Bhatia plus Marshall–Olkin–Arnold for majorization, invariant norms, and principal
   angles.
3. Conway and Reed–Simon for Hilbert-space polar and spectral theory.
4. Pietsch and Simon for approximation numbers and operator ideals.
5. Davis–Kahan, Rosenblum, and Bhatia–Davis–McIntosh for spectral-subspace
   perturbation.
6. Berge/Aliprantis–Border and Tropp/Vershynin for the optimization and probability
   arm.

The child roadmap `References` sections remain the authoritative record of which
source supports a particular milestone. This document is the consolidated navigation
layer.
