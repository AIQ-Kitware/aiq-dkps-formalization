## 4. Verification protocol — do not shortcut these

- **One Lean-touching process at a time.** `lake build`, the census gate and the
  frontier gate all shell out to Lean and share one `.lake`. Two at once fake
  `.olean`-missing regressions. This has bitten the campaign three times.
- **Never pipe a build.** `lake build | tail` returns *tail's* exit code. Use
  `{ lake build > log 2>&1; echo "BUILD_EXIT=$?" >> log; }` and **wait for that
  line** (`until grep -q BUILD_EXIT log; do sleep 30; done`). **Do not read exit
  status from a task notification** — one fired "exit code 0" while `lake` was
  still running, because the wrapper shell had exited; the real line landed nine
  minutes later.
- **Axiom probe from outside the source tree**, importing `DavisKahan.All`, with
  `example : True := trivial` as a sentinel — without it, a failure at the import
  line runs no `#print axioms` and reads as success. Expect exactly
  `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. **Include positive controls**: a
  closure or probe reporting zeros proves nothing unless it also shows it reached
  the region. For dependency-closure checks use `allowOpaque := true` — without
  it a types-only closure of 3291 constants looked clean where the real traversal
  reaches 56793.
- **Non-vacuity.** When work claims a statement now holds over ℝ, force a
  `#check` at a genuinely real space (`EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 3)`), not a
  `RealComplexification`.
- Regenerate `dev/audit/FILE-CHECKLIST.md` (`scripts/audit_checklist.py`, git-only,
  safe beside a build) after every integration; it drifts silently.

## 7. The single most useful habit to carry forward

Three consecutive dispatch briefs of mine carried a **load-bearing false
premise**, and every one was caught by the subagent, never by a gate — because
the brief is the only artifact in the loop with no checker. One of them would
have retired a census blocker on an incomplete row and reported it done.

Two cheap countermeasures, both with a perfect record here:

1. Put in every brief: *"report every false premise you find in this brief — it
   is as valuable as the proofs."* Subagents will not volunteer corrections to
   their instructions unless invited; when invited, they lead with them.
2. Before asserting a clause is already covered, **enumerate the row's own
   `lean_declarations` and check each printed clause against them** — rather than
   reasoning from what the previous mission reported. That is free.
